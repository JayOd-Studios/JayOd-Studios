03 — Questions

The things
everyone asks first.

Straight answers, before you spend a call finding out.

A focused marketing site usually lands between $2,000 and $5,000. A web app or store gets scoped after one short call. Either way you get a fixed price in writing before any code is written, and it doesn’t move unless the scope does.

Three to five weeks for a marketing site; six to ten for an app or an online store. A staging link goes live in week one so you watch it come together instead of waiting for a reveal at the end.

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript for sites like this one — it’s faster and lasts longer than a page builder. React and TypeScript when the project genuinely needs state, and Shopify or WordPress when you need to edit things yourself after launch.

You do. The repository, the assets and a short written guide to running it are all part of handover. There’s no licence fee, no locked platform and no arrangement where you have to come back to us to change a phone number.

04 — Approach

Four steps, no mystery

You’ll know the price, the pages and the timeline before any code gets written.

  1. 01

    Scope

    A short call, then a written scope: pages, features, timeline and a fixed price. If the brief isn’t a fit, we say so.

  2. 02

    Design

    Structure first, then a designed direction in the browser — not a static mockup that falls apart once it’s real.

  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-written, responsive front-end with accessible components. A staging link from week one so you watch it come together.

  4. 04

    Launch & care

    Hosting, analytics, redirects and a handover you can follow. Then thirty days of support while things settle.

05 — Studio

Small on purpose.

JayOd Studios is an independent web development studio founded in 2026. No account managers, no hand-off to a team you never meet — the person who scopes your project is the person who builds it.

We started from a simple frustration: too many small businesses pay agency prices for templated work, then get left with a site nobody can maintain. We do the opposite. Short scopes, honest timelines, clean code and a handover you can use.

Being early also means being available. Our first clients get our full attention, our best work and a rate that won’t exist once the portfolio is full.
